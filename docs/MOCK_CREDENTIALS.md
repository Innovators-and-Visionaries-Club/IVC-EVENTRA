## 3. How to Trigger Data Seeding

You can populate or sync all 5 Judges, 5 SPOCs, 5 Club Members, and 20 Teams (60 Participants) at any time:
1. Navigate to **`/admin/users`** in the Admin Portal.
2. Click the **`Seed All Portals Data`** button at the top right.
3. The server action creates any missing auth accounts, sets profiles, provisions teams, creates member associations, and allots 4 teams per SPOC and 4 teams per Judge automatically!
